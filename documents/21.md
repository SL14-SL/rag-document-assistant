## S1: Tickets

Closed tickets are deleted after 365 calendar days by default. A workspace administrator can shorten this period to 90 or 180 days. Open tickets are not deleted under this retention rule.

## S2: Backups

Backup copies are overwritten after 30 calendar days. A deleted ticket may therefore remain temporarily in backups. This document does not promise restoration of individual tickets.
