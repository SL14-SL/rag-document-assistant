## S1: Reporting

Suspicious sign-ins, lost company devices and confidential data accidentally shared externally must be reported to Security immediately by telephone. Do not wait for the next team meeting. Record the time and observed facts.

## S2: Initial actions

If malware is suspected, disconnect the device from the network but do not wipe it yourself. Security coordinates further action. Reporting does not replace the need to revoke affected credentials.
