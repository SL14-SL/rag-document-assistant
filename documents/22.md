## S1: Requirements

A production change requires an approved pull request, successful automated checks and a documented rollback option. The author of the code must not be its sole approver.

## S2: Deployment window

Regular deployments take place on Tuesdays and Thursdays between 10:00 and 14:00 German local time. Emergency changes outside that window require incident lead approval and a retrospective review on the next working day.
