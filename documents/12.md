## S1: Travel request

Every business trip requires manager approval in the service portal before booking. The request must include purpose, dates, destination and estimated total cost. Any hotel rate above the limit must be explicitly identified.

## S2: Booking

Rail travel is normally booked in second class. Personal extensions must be accounted for separately. Non-refundable bookings are permitted only after all required approvals have been obtained.
