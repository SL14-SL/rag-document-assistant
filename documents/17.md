## S1: Hours

Regular support is available Monday to Friday, 09:00 to 17:00 German local time, excluding public holidays in Hamburg. Outside this window, P1 on-call support is available only to customers with an on-call support agreement.

## S2: Escalation

A P1 is immediately escalated to the incident lead on duty. A P2 is escalated to the support lead after one working day without progress. Priority definitions are in document NL-16.
