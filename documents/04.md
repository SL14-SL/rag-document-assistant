## S1: Request

Requests must specify the application, required role, business purpose and desired end date. The manager approves the business need and the application owner approves the role. IT Operations implements access only after both approvals.

## S2: Time limits

Administrator privileges are granted for no more than seven calendar days and require a new request afterwards. Shared user accounts are prohibited. Standard access is disabled at 18:00 German local time on the final working day when an employee leaves.
