## S1: Permissions

Readers can view tickets. Editors can view, create and edit tickets. Administrators can also manage users and settings. Only administrators may initiate a full workspace export.

## S2: Assignment

The workspace administrator assigns new roles. Employees also need the process in document NL-04 to access the internal company instance. Product roles do not replace internal approval.
