## S1: Hotel costs

For business travel within Germany, the standard hotel limit is EUR 120 gross per night, including breakfast. A higher rate requires written manager approval before booking. Document NL-11 also applies during trade fairs.

## S2: Expense claims

Receipts must be submitted within 30 calendar days after the trip ends. This policy does not specify reimbursement rules for childcare associated with business travel. This version fully replaces version 1.4.
