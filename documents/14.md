## S1: Classes

The classification levels are public, internal and confidential. Customer contracts, credentials and personal customer data are confidential. Published product brochures are public. Internal process descriptions are internal.

## S2: Handling

Confidential data may be stored only in approved company systems. Sharing it with external recipients requires data owner approval. Publicly accessible AI services are not an approved storage location for confidential data.
