## S1: Before joining

People Operations opens an onboarding ticket at least five working days before the start date. The manager specifies the role, team and required applications. IT Operations initially provisions standard access only.

## S2: First week

On the first working day, identity is verified and multi-factor authentication is configured. Security induction must be completed within the first five working days. Additional application permissions require the process in document NL-04.
