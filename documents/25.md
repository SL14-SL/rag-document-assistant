## S1: Thresholds

One-off purchases up to and including EUR 250 net require manager approval. Higher amounts also require Finance approval. Recurring software subscriptions require approval from Finance and IT Operations regardless of amount.

## S2: Ordering

Place orders only after approval. Personal advance payments do not create an automatic right to reimbursement. Business travel is governed separately by documents NL-09, NL-11 and NL-12.
