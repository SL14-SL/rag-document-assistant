## S1: Sign-in

Multi-factor authentication is mandatory for the central company account. Passwords must contain at least 14 characters and must not be reused for personal services. Credentials are stored in the approved password manager.

## S2: Lost authentication factor

If a second authentication factor is lost, contact the Service Desk by telephone. IT Operations resets the factor after identity verification. Never disclose an MFA code, even to someone claiming to work for IT.
