# Trade Fair Hotel Exception

Document ID: NL-11
Version: 1.0
Status: active
Owner: Finance
Effective from: 2026-10-01

Synthetic example document; not a real company policy.

## S1: Exception

If no suitable hotel within the standard limit is available at the destination during a trade fair, up to EUR 180 gross per night including breakfast may be approved. This is not an automatic increase in the hotel limit.

## S2: Evidence

Two price quotations and the trade fair dates must be recorded in the travel request before booking. Finance must approve in writing in addition to the manager. Without both approvals, the standard limit in document NL-09 applies.
