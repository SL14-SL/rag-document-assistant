## S1: Classification

P1 means a complete production outage affecting multiple customers with no workaround. P2 means a significant impairment with a workaround. P3 covers normal defects and general requests.

## S2: Initial response

During support hours, initial response targets are 30 minutes for P1, four hours for P2 and two working days for P3. Initial response means acknowledgement and initial assessment, not full resolution.
