## S1: Workation

Workation is permitted only within the EU for a maximum of ten working days per calendar year. Requests must be submitted at least 15 working days before the start date. Approval is not guaranteed.

## S2: Approvals

Both the manager and People Operations must give written approval before travel. Working from abroad is not permitted without both approvals. Document NL-06 alone does not constitute workation approval.
