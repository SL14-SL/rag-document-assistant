## S1: Allowance

Up to three remote-working days per calendar week are permitted with manager agreement. Required team meetings on site take priority. Unused remote-working days cannot be carried over to the following week.

## S2: Workspace

Screens must be protected from viewing by third parties. Access to internal systems from outside company locations requires the company VPN. Working from another country is also subject to document NL-07.
