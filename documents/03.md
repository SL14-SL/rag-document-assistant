## S1: Devices

Standard equipment includes a managed laptop, a headset and one external monitor. Personal devices must not access internal customer data. The laptop remains company property.

## S2: Orders and returns

Additional hardware requires manager approval. Faulty equipment is reported through the service portal. Departing employees must return all devices to IT Operations by their final working day.
