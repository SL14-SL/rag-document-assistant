## S1: Collaboration

Shared availability hours are Monday to Friday, 10:00 to 15:00 German local time. Individual part-time agreements take precedence. Working time must be recorded daily in the time-tracking system.

## S2: Absence

Planned absences are entered in the team calendar. In the event of illness, notify the manager before the usual start of work. This document does not specify the annual leave entitlement.
